Evidence snapshot through 27 September 2026. L40S energy revalidation trials from 28 September are not included.

How to read this page: each study (phase) is broken into one row-group per workload/model it was tested on. The grey chips above each table are the parameters that stayed fixed for every condition in that group — the blue chips and the table's own columns are the parameters that were deliberately changed between conditions. Click any row (or a group's “Compare all” link) to open the full measured detail, including results by platform. Configured means no completed trial is recorded yet; Deferred means the condition is on hold for another reason. OOM-limited means a CUDA out-of-memory failure was measured on the named GPU at the tested settings. It is a ceiling for that configuration, not proof that a smaller batch or a different method cannot run. An experiment may be completed on H100 but OOM-limited on L40S. Failed attempts do not count as completed performance results.
